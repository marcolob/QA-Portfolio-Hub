Compliance Case Study – Regulated Product Scenario

Project Overview

This case study demonstrates structured compliance readiness for a simulated regulated product within iGaming. The goal was to map regulatory requirements to testing processes, ensuring traceability and audit-ready documentation.

Objectives

  • Translate regulatory requirements into actionable compliance checklists.
  • Create traceability between requirements, test cases, and evidence.
  • Document audit-ready outputs suitable for regulatory review.

Approach

  • Extracted requirements from MGA, UKGC, and GDPR documentation.
  • Designed compliance checklists and mapped to test executions.
  • Captured supporting evidence (screenshots, logs) for all tests.
  • Prepared a summary compliance report for internal audit purposes.

Outcome

The project ensured full traceability of regulatory requirements to testing activities and audit-ready documentation. This approach demonstrates readiness for Technical Compliance and Product Certification roles.

Tools & Skills Demonstrated

  • Regulatory analysis: MGA, UKGC, GDPR
  • Traceability matrices & audit documentation
  • Jira & Confluence for documentation and issue tracking
  • Cross-functional collaboration with QA and Product teams
  • Attention to detail, reporting, and compliance process management
